# Purpose

The Product Roadmap defines the long-term evolution of Kitchen Brain.

It provides a structured development plan that aligns product vision, architecture and implementation.

The roadmap ensures every sprint contributes towards a clearly defined product goal rather than becoming an isolated feature release.

# Version 1.2 — Smart Kitchen

## Vision

Reduce manual kitchen management through smarter automation.

---

## Theme

Smart Kitchen

---

## Sprint 18

### Smart Pantry

Goal

Reduce household food waste.

Features

- Expiry Tracking
- Purchase Date
- Shelf Life
- Storage Locations
- Pantry Health
- Pantry Intelligence
- Use Soon Recommendations
- Smart Expiry Badges

Success Criteria

Kitchen Brain can identify ingredients that require immediate attention.

---

## Sprint 19

### Better Grocery Engine

Goal

Improve shopping accuracy.

Features

- Duplicate Prevention
- Quantity Recommendations
- Pantry-aware Shopping
- Shopping History
- Smart Reordering
- Grocery Optimization

Success Criteria

Generated grocery lists require minimal manual editing.

---

## Sprint 20

### Search & Discovery

Goal

Help users quickly locate information.

Features

- Global Search
- Pantry Search
- Recipe Search
- Planner Search
- Smart Filters
- Fast Results

Success Criteria

Users can locate information from anywhere within seconds.

---

## Sprint 21

### Budget Intelligence

Goal

Provide better financial awareness.

Features

- Spending Trends
- Budget Forecasting
- Cost Per Meal
- Pantry Valuation
- Waste Cost Estimation

Success Criteria

Users understand household grocery spending at a glance.

---

## Version 1.2 Exit Criteria

Version 1.2 is complete when:

- Pantry becomes proactive.
- Grocery becomes predictive.
- Search is available throughout the application.
- Budget provides actionable insights.

---

## Out of Scope

The following features are intentionally excluded:

- AI Conversations
- Voice Assistant
- Barcode Scanner
- OCR
- Family Collaboration

These belong to future versions.

# Release Principles

Every release should improve at least one of the following:

- Smarter Decisions
- Better User Experience
- Higher Performance
- Better Integration

# Long-Term Vision

Kitchen Brain should evolve from a kitchen management application into an intelligent household platform that actively helps families make better daily decisions.

Future versions should continue reducing manual effort while increasing personalization and intelligence.
